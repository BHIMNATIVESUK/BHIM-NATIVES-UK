THE JOURNEY

The STONEBWOY story

From Ghana to stages around the world, this is a look at the journey behind the name.

STONEBWOY, born Livingstone Etse Satekla, is a Ghanaian artist whose sound connects reggae, dancehall, Afrobeats and African influences.

His rise became especially visible during the 2010s, when major Ghanaian recognition was followed by international attention.

In 2015, STONEBWOY won the BET Award for Best International Act: Africa, becoming one of the major Ghanaian artists to receive international recognition at the ceremony.

Since then, his story has continued through albums, singles, international collaborations, tours, festivals and major performances.

The BHIM movement has grown alongside the music, creating a worldwide community connected through culture, sound and identity.

RECOGNITION

Awards & Achievements

Tap Read More and the story opens directly on BHIM Natives UK.

2015 • BET AWARDS

Best International Act: Africa

One of the defining international breakthroughs of the career.

In 2015, STONEBWOY won the BET Award for Best International Act: Africa. The achievement became a major international milestone and helped place Ghanaian reggae and dancehall on an even bigger global stage.
2021 • AFRIMA

African Reggae, Ragga & Dancehall

Another major continental recognition.

At the 2021 AFRIMA, STONEBWOY won Best Artiste, Duo or Group — African Reggae, Ragga & Dancehall. The award was part of his continuing recognition across African music.
2024 • TELECEL GHANA MUSIC AWARDS

Artiste of the Year

A major home-country achievement.

STONEBWOY won Artiste of the Year at the 25th Telecel Ghana Music Awards. He also received multiple additional honours, making the ceremony one of the major award nights of the period.
2024 • 3MUSIC AWARDS

Artiste of the Year

Another important Ghanaian award-show victory.

At the 2024 3Music Awards, STONEBWOY won Artiste of the Year, Best Reggae/Dancehall Act and Album of the Year for 5th Dimension.
