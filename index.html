THE JOURNEY

The STONEBWOY Story

From Ashaiman to international stages, this is the story of Livingstone Etse Satekla — the artist the world knows as STONEBWOY.

Livingstone Etse Satekla, professionally known as STONEBWOY, is a Ghanaian singer, songwriter and record producer associated with reggae, dancehall, Afrobeats and Afropop.

His musical journey developed in Ashaiman, Ghana, and his early breakthrough came through songs including "Climax" and "Ghetto Love" in 2012.

His career continued through albums including Grade 1, Necessary Evil, Epistles of Mama, Anloga Junction, 5th Dimension, UP & RUNNIN6, THE TORCHER and TORCHER II.

International recognition followed, including a BET Award in 2015 and Billboard chart history through Epistles of Mama.

But the story is bigger than awards. It is a story of resilience, identity, Ghanaian culture, music, family, ambition and a global community known as BHIM Nation.

CHAPTER 01 • CHILDHOOD

Growing Up In Ashaiman

The environment that became part of the foundation of STONEBWOY's identity.

Before international stages, awards and albums, there was Livingstone Etse Satekla and the environment that helped shape his outlook. STONEBWOY is closely associated with Ashaiman in Ghana, a place that has remained important to the identity surrounding his music and public story.

Growing up in Ghana exposed him to different sounds, languages, cultures and everyday experiences that would later become important ingredients in his artistry. His music would eventually move between dancehall, reggae, Afrobeats and other African sounds, while retaining a strong Ghanaian identity.

The early years matter because they provide context for the artist audiences see today. The international performer was not created overnight. His identity developed through experiences, influences and an increasing desire to express himself through music.

STONEBWOY's story is therefore not simply a story about becoming famous. It is a story about developing a voice and carrying that voice from a Ghanaian community to audiences around the world.

CHAPTER 02 • BEGINNINGS

How The Music Started

From early songwriting and performances to the first major breakthrough.

STONEBWOY's relationship with music developed long before international recognition. His story includes early songwriting and performance experiences before he began establishing himself professionally.

Apple Music identifies 2012 as a major early breakthrough, with "Climax" and "Ghetto Love" helping introduce his name to a wider audience. Both songs were nominated for Ghana Music Awards.

That period represented the beginning of a career that would eventually stretch across multiple albums, international collaborations and performances around the world.

The early chapter is important because it shows how the foundations of the artist were already present before the international spotlight arrived. What started as a Ghanaian music career would gradually become an international African music story.

CHAPTER 03 • RESILIENCE

The Accident That Changed His Life

A difficult chapter that became part of the story of STONEBWOY's resilience.

One of the most difficult chapters in STONEBWOY's life came when he was involved in a serious road accident during his youth. The experience became part of the personal story he has discussed publicly over the years.

The accident affected his physical wellbeing and created challenges that extended beyond the immediate injuries. His recovery and the support of his family became important parts of his journey.

For an artist whose career would later depend heavily on performance, movement and stage presence, dealing with physical limitations created another obstacle to overcome.

Rather than becoming the end of his ambitions, the experience became part of a larger story of resilience. The road from injury to international performer required patience, determination and continued belief in his future.

This chapter is important because it adds another dimension to the STONEBWOY story. Behind the artist known for energetic performances is a person who has experienced difficult moments and continued moving forward.

CHAPTER 04 • PURPOSE

Why STONEBWOY Makes Music

Music as identity, expression, culture and connection.

For STONEBWOY, music has become much more than a career. His work brings together African identity, dancehall culture, reggae, Afrobeats and personal experiences.

His catalogue regularly moves between messages of resilience, love, ambition, spirituality, celebration and social awareness. This allows different audiences to find different meanings in the same body of work.

His international journey has also given him an opportunity to represent Ghana and African music outside the continent. Collaborations with artists from different countries have helped place his sound inside a much wider musical conversation.

At the centre is communication. Music allows STONEBWOY to speak to people who may live thousands of miles away but recognise something in the emotions, rhythms and stories contained in the songs.

CHAPTER 05 • COMMUNITY

The Rise Of BHIM Nation

How an artist's fan identity became part of his international story.

BHIM Nation is an important part of the STONEBWOY story. It represents the community of supporters connected with his music, identity and journey.

The name has grown alongside the artist's career, appearing across concerts, festivals, fan communities and international events.

BHIM Nation also reflects the relationship between an African artist and a worldwide audience. Fans can be thousands of miles away from Ghana while still feeling connected to the culture and music.

BHIM Festival has become one of the major events associated with this community, giving fans an opportunity to experience the music collectively.

For BHIM Natives UK, documenting this community is part of the purpose of the website: celebrating the people, music and moments that make the BHIM Nation story bigger than a single stage.

CHAPTER 06 • GLOBAL

From Ghana To The World

How the music moved from Ashaiman to international audiences.

STONEBWOY's international journey has been built through music, collaborations, awards, touring and consistent engagement with audiences outside Ghana.

Apple Music notes major international milestones including the 2015 BET Award for Best International Act and the Billboard chart appearance of Epistles of Mama.

His collaborations have also connected him with artists from Africa, the Caribbean, Europe and the United States. This reflects the broad musical language of his Afro-dancehall style.

International touring has become another important part of the story. Performing in different countries allows the music to move from streaming platforms into real-world communities.

The global story is ultimately about connection: Ghanaian roots remaining visible while the music continues travelling across borders.

2015 • BET AWARDS

Best International Act: Africa

A defining international breakthrough for STONEBWOY and Ghanaian music.

The 2015 BET Award for Best International Act: Africa became one of the defining moments of STONEBWOY's early international career.

The recognition placed his name in a global conversation and demonstrated the growing international visibility of Ghanaian reggae and dancehall.

It was an important milestone in the journey from Ghanaian stages to international recognition.

BILLBOARD • EPISTLES OF MAMA

Billboard World Albums

A major chart milestone for Ghanaian music.

According to Apple Music's artist biography, Epistles of Mama debuted at No. 13 on the Billboard World Albums chart.

The achievement made STONEBWOY the first Ghanaian artist to land on a Billboard chart, adding another important chapter to his international story.

2023 • 5TH DIMENSION

International Album Milestones

The album reached audiences across multiple international markets.

5th Dimension expanded the musical palette of STONEBWOY's catalogue while maintaining the Afro-dancehall identity associated with his work.

The album included collaborations with artists including Stormzy, Angelique Kidjo, Davido, Tiwa Savage, Oxlade and Shaggy.

2025 WORLD TOUR

UP & RUNNIN6 World Tour

The tour supported the 2024 album and included North American dates across the United States and Canada, followed by UK and European shows.

The UP & RUNNIN6 touring era extended the life of the album beyond streaming platforms and into live performance.

The tour continued a pattern that has become central to STONEBWOY's career: taking a new project to international audiences and turning songs into live experiences.
